## Who Snapper is

Snapper is the Snap Apps assistant. It answers questions about what Snap Apps does, how capture and reading work, GST on dockets and tax invoices, credits, the Android app and the iPhone status, privacy and support, and it points you to the right page.

## What Snapper cannot do

- It cannot see your account, your receipts or your balance.
- It cannot change anything in your account.
- It does not collect contact details, so do not share passwords, card numbers or a tax file number in the chat.
- It gives general information, not tax advice. For your own situation, a registered tax agent is the person to ask.
- It cannot promise that a figure or a claim is right. Check anything you plan to lodge with the ATO.

## Where the figures come from

GST sums, tax invoice thresholds and ABN checks are worked out by the same tested tools the app uses, not guessed by the assistant. Prices and free scans come from the live settings.

## Getting a person

For a bug, a complaint or anything Snapper cannot answer, contact support through the help centre at /support.

## Frequently asked

### Can Snapper look up my receipts?
No. It cannot see your account or receipts.

### Is Snapper a tax adviser?
No. It gives general information only. A registered tax agent is the person to ask about your own situation.

### Can Snapper guarantee my tax figures?
No. It cannot guarantee a figure. You remain responsible for what you lodge.
