## Where to get help

The help centre at /support answers the common questions, with a search box and sections for capture problems, corrections, GST and tax invoices, credits and pricing, and data export and retention. The guides at /docs explain how the app works day to day.

## Contacting support

If you cannot find your answer, email support@snapapps.com.au. Say what you were doing and, if it is about a specific document, its date and supplier so it can be found quickly.

## How quickly will I hear back?

Support is best-effort, typically within 3 business days. The docs and the help centre search cover most questions faster.

## Account and data requests

To delete your account, delete your YourTal account. Your Snap Apps data goes with it within 72 hours. Access to your information and corrections go through support. Security issues are reported there too.

## Frequently asked

### How do I talk to a person?
Email support@snapapps.com.au or use the help centre at /support.

### Where are the guides?
At /docs: getting started, capturing receipts, reviewing corrections, confidence and flags, the ledger, and data retention.

### Can I delete my account?
Yes. Delete your YourTal account and your Snap Apps data, including your receipts, is deleted within 72 hours. Export your receipts first if you need a copy. A shared workspace keeps its records, with your name removed.
