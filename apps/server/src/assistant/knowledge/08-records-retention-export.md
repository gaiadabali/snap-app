## The five-year rule

The ATO requires most business records to be kept for five years from when they were prepared or obtained, or from when the transaction was completed, whichever is later. Snap Apps keeps the original photo and what was read from it together as a true copy for that period.

## Why the original is kept as taken

The ATO accepts an electronic copy only where it is a true and clear reproduction of the original. So the original is never edited, recompressed or overwritten. A separate normalised copy is made for reading.

## After the five years

Once a record's retention period has passed, an automated job removes it rather than keeping it indefinitely.

## Deleting your account

If you delete your YourTal account, your Snap Apps data is deleted too, including your receipts, within 72 hours. Export your receipts as a CSV first if you need a copy.

A shared workspace you belong to keeps its records, with your name removed.

## CSV export

Export your receipts as a CSV any time, from the Spending screen. You can take one row per receipt or one row per line item, and any spreadsheet opens the file.

## Frequently asked

### How long are receipts kept?
Five years, the ATO's record-keeping period.

### Can I get my data out?
Yes. Export your receipts as a CSV whenever you like.

### Can I delete my account?
Yes. Delete your YourTal account and your Snap Apps data, including your receipts, is deleted within 72 hours. Export your receipts as a CSV first if you need a copy. A shared workspace keeps its records, with your name removed.
