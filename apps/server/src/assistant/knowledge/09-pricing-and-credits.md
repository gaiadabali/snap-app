## Pay for scans, not subscriptions

A new account starts with {{FREE_SCANS}} free scans. After those, a scan costs one credit, and each credit is {{PRICE_PER_SCAN}}. GST and card fees are already in that rate, so nothing is added at the end. Nothing renews.

## Credit packs

Credits come in packs of {{PACK_SIZES}}. Every pack is the same rate per scan: double the scans and you double the cost, nothing more. There is no volume discount to chase and no pack that is the wrong one to buy.

## Credits never expire

Credits are not a monthly allowance and there is no period for them to reset at. Buy a pack and use it over a month or over a year. The free scans a new account starts with do not expire either.

## What counts as a scan

One document, read end to end: every line, the GST split and the checks. That costs one credit. It is a document, not a page and not a call to a server.

## One credit buys the whole read

There is no base fee with extras on top. A bigger pack buys more scans, not more features. Every scan includes line-by-line reading, the GST split, tax invoice and ABN checks, five years as a true copy, and CSV export.

## Buying credits is not open yet

The checkout is not connected to a payment processor, so there is nothing to pay with today. You can create an account and use your free scans now. The buy-credits screen says plainly that there is nothing to pay with.

## Frequently asked

### Is there a subscription?
No. There is no subscription, no contract and no minimum. Nothing recurs, so there is nothing to cancel.

### Do credits expire?
No. Credits never expire and never reset.

### Can I buy credits yet?
Not yet. Buying is not open, because the checkout has no payment processor connected.

### Is GST included in the price?
Yes. GST and the card fee are already inside the price.

### What happens when my free scans run out?
Nothing is dropped. The photo is still kept, and it is read once there is a credit to read it with.

### Can a household share credits?
Yes. Credits belong to the workspace, so everyone in the household draws from one balance.
