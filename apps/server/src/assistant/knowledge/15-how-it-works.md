## Five stops

Take one photo and Snap Apps keeps it as taken, reads every line, checks the tax invoice rules and files the result.

## 1. One photo, kept exactly as taken

The file is stored byte for byte as it left the camera and is never edited. Everything afterwards works on a separate working copy.

## 2. Queued on the phone

With no signal, the photo waits in a queue on your phone and goes up when the connection returns. Each send carries a one-off key so the same docket is never counted twice.

## 3. Every line read

Each line gets its item, its price and whether it carries GST. The reading is saved as a numbered version of the photo. When a figure is too smudged to trust, the app leaves it blank and flags it for you.

## 4. The tax invoice rules

Is it marked as a tax invoice, does the ABN pass the checksum, is the GST exactly one eleventh of the taxable lines, do the lines add up? Above a set amount the buyer must be identified too. If a rule fails you get the reason and a suggested fix.

## 5. Filed and balanced

The docket lands in your spending under its category. Money is kept as exact decimals, and every entry is paired so the books add up to zero.

## Getting started

Photograph a docket, wait for the reading, and review it if anything is flagged. The review screen shows what failed. Confirming a document is the deliberate step that files it.

## Frequently asked

### How long does reading take?
The docket is read after it uploads. The site does not promise a specific time.

### What happens if it cannot read a figure?
It leaves the field blank and flags it for you to fill in. It does not make up a number.
