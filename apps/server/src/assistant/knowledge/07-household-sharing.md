## One set of budgets

Share a workspace with your partner or the whole household, and everyone works from the same budgets. There are no screenshots and no "what's left this month?" texts. The number is the same on both phones.

## Owner and Member

The person who sets the household up is its Owner. Everyone they invite is a Member.

## Credits are shared too

Credits belong to the workspace, not to a person. Everyone in the household who scans in that workspace draws from the same balance, so there is one top-up rather than one each.

## Frequently asked

### Can my partner and I share budgets?
Yes. Share a workspace and both of you see the same budgets.

### Who is the Owner?
The person who sets the household up. People they invite are Members.

### Do we each need our own credits?
No. Credits belong to the workspace, so everyone in it draws from one balance.
