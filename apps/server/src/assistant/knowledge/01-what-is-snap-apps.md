## What Snap Apps is

Snap Apps is a receipt app that reads every line. You photograph a docket and Snap Apps reads each item and its price, splits GST-free lines from taxable ones, checks whether it is a valid tax invoice, and keeps a true copy of the original for five years. Then it shows you where the money went.

It is built for Australians who want their receipts sorted properly, rather than a pile of photos in a camera roll.

## What you get today

The app ships as the personal release. That means:

- every line of a docket read and priced, not just the total
- the GST-free and taxable split worked out line by line
- tax invoice and ABN checks, with a suggested fix when a docket falls short
- the original photo kept untouched as a true copy for five years
- offline capture, which uploads when you are back in range
- spending and analytics, budgets, recurring payments and savings goals
- household sharing, so everyone sees the same budgets
- CSV export

## Who it is for

Anyone who keeps receipts and wants them read accurately: households tracking a budget, people who want to know what GST was really charged, and anyone who has lost a docket at the worst moment.

## What is not in the app today

The BAS report, tax pack, deduction estimates, invoices, bills, mileage tracking, Xero connection and accounting practice tools are not available in the app today.

## Frequently asked

### Is Snap Apps free to try?
Yes. Every new account starts with {{FREE_SCANS}} free scans, and you do not need a card to sign up.

### Where can I use it?
There is an Android app you can download directly, and the web app works in any browser. The iPhone app is in development and cannot be installed yet.

### Does Snap Apps give tax advice?
No. It gives general information and checks dockets against published rules. For your own situation, a registered tax agent is the person to ask.
