## Auto-capture

Point the camera at a docket and Snap Apps finds the edges of the paper and takes the shot when you hold steady. Before a photo is uploaded, the app checks on your phone that it is not blurry and that it looks like a document, so an obvious retake is caught on the spot rather than after a wait.

## The original is kept untouched

The photo you take is stored exactly as it left the camera, byte for byte. Reading never edits it. Everything the app does afterwards works on a separate working copy, so the original stays a true copy of the docket in your hand.

## Works with no signal

No signal at the truck stop is fine. The photo waits in a queue on your phone and uploads when you are back in range. Each send carries a one-off key, so a retry, a double tap or a dropped connection never counts the same docket twice. The docket is read once it has uploaded.

## Photos and PDFs

Already have the receipt as a photo, or as a PDF in your email? Add it the same way as a fresh snap.

## Tips for a good read

- Lay the receipt flat. A curled thermal receipt distorts the totals.
- Get the whole docket in frame, including the top and the bottom line.
- Avoid glare across the total.
- One receipt per photo, unless it is genuinely one multi-page document. A multi-page tax invoice should go in as a single document so the lines reconcile against one total.
- Thermal receipts fade. Photograph fuel and servo dockets as soon as you can.

## Duplicates

Uploading the exact same image twice is recognised straight away and treated as one capture. A docket that only looks like another (same shop, same amount, same day) is flagged for you to check, never merged automatically, because two identical purchases on one day can both be real.

## Frequently asked

### Does it work offline?
Yes. You can capture with no signal. The app keeps the photo on your phone and uploads it when you are back online, and it is read then.

### Can I add a PDF?
Yes. Photos and PDFs are both supported.

### The app keeps asking me to retake the photo. Why?
The quality check rejects a blurry shot or one that does not look like a document. Lay the receipt flat, avoid glare and get the whole receipt in frame.
