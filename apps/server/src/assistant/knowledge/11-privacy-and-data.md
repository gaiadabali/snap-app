## Where your data is stored

Your account, your records and the original images of your receipts are stored on a server operated for Snap Apps by Hostinger in Jakarta, Indonesia.

## How receipts are read

To read a receipt, a copy of its image is sent to OpenRouter, Inc. in the United States, which passes it to the provider running the language model that reads it. That provider may be in the United States or another country. Each processes the image under its own terms.

Both are disclosures of personal information outside Australia, and APP 8 of the Privacy Act applies to them. Moving storage and receipt reading to Australia (Sydney) is planned. Until that happens, the arrangement above is the one in use.

## Reading on your phone

When the Android app reads a receipt on the device itself, it uses Google's ML Kit text recogniser. Your photo and the text read from it stay on your phone, and ML Kit does not send the image or its result to Google. Google does receive usage and performance metrics about the recogniser, which describe how it ran, not what your receipt says. A device without Google Play services simply uses the server reading described above.

## What Snap Apps does not do

It does not sell your data, and it does not use your financial data to sell you advertising. Full card numbers are not stored: a card number on a receipt is reduced to the last four digits and the card brand. GPS location in photo metadata is stripped from the copy used for processing and is never indexed.

## Your rights

You can ask for access to the personal information held about you, ask for a correction, or ask for your account to be deleted, through support. Records inside the five-year retention window are closed out of active use but kept until the window passes. If you are unhappy with how a privacy concern is handled, you can complain to the OAIC.

The privacy policy is marked as a draft and is due a legal review.

## Frequently asked

### Where is my data stored?
On a server in Jakarta, Indonesia, operated for Snap Apps by Hostinger.

### Does my receipt leave Australia?
Yes. Your data is stored in Jakarta, and a copy of a receipt image is sent to OpenRouter in the United States to be read. Both are APP 8 disclosures. Sydney is planned.

### Does Google see my receipts?
No. Reading on your phone uses ML Kit, and the image and text stay on the device. Google only receives usage and performance metrics about the recogniser.

### Do you sell my data?
No.
