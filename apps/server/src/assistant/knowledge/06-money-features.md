## Spending and analytics

Every receipt you snap is filed under a category and counted in your spending. See the month by week and by category, and search by merchant, category or amount.

## Budgets and safe to spend a day

Set a budget and Snap Apps shows what is left to spend. It also works out a safe-to-spend figure for each day, based on what is left in the budget and how many days remain in the month. The figure stays current as you scan.

## Recurring

Subscriptions and standing bills are found in your receipts, so you can see what keeps coming out.

## Savings goals

Put money toward something and each goal shows how far along it is.

## Everything after the snap

Reading the docket is the first job. These features are what you do with a month of them: where the money went, what is left and which receipts need a second look.

## Frequently asked

### What is safe to spend a day?
It is what is left in your budget spread across the days left in the month. It updates as you scan.

### Can I search my receipts?
Yes. Search by merchant, category or amount.

### Does Snap Apps find my subscriptions?
It finds recurring payments and standing bills in your receipts and lists them as recurring.
